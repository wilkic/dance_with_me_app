Privacy

Dance With Me · dance.wilkinscoffee.be · Last updated 18 October 2026

We store nothing about you.
  • No accounts, no recordings.
  • Your camera image never leaves your phone. It's only used there to draw your stick figure.
  • While you're in a room, your stick figure and any music you share are passed live to the others in that room. Nothing is saved.
  • No tracking or advertising cookies.

What happens live

To let people dance together, our server relays your stick-figure movement to everyone in your room, and works out your tempo from it, in real time. When you leave, it's gone. Only dance on camera with people in your space who are happy to be there.

Music played to a room goes straight from the host's phone to each dancer's phone. Because those phones connect directly, they can see each other's IP address. To set up that connection, phones also ask Google's public STUN server (stun.l.google.com) for their own public address.

What our servers see

Like any website, our servers see your IP address to connect you. The dance server doesn't log it. Its system logs (start-ups, errors) are wiped after 3 days.

The website itself is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), which, like any web host, sees your IP address when you load the page; see GitHub's privacy statement. The dance server (rooms, guests, tempo) runs on Oracle Cloud in Frankfurt, Germany.

On your device

Your settings, like guest opacity and AI variation, are saved in your own browser so they're there next time. If you book a DJ set, your browser also keeps a key that lets you follow and cancel your booking. You can clear all of it by clearing your browser data.

DJ bookings

If you book a room, we keep what you put in the form (DJ name, set info, about you, link). Once a booking is approved, it's shown publicly on the start page. When your set is over, all of it is deleted. Requests that aren't approved are deleted when their time has passed.

Music

Please don't share music you don't own. You're responsible for what you broadcast. To report a room, email Chris@wilkinscoffee.be.
