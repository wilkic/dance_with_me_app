How keys work

A dance key lets you record clips of your dancing in the app. There is no account: the key is the only link between you and your clips, so we can't look it up for you if you lose it.

Under a key we keep the clips you recorded (your stick figure's movement as points, and the beat of the music, never the camera picture or the sound) and the dates the key was made and last used.

A key that isn't used for 30 days is deleted by itself, with everything under it. Deleting here is immediate.
