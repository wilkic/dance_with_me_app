Three ways to draw a dancer

The same dancer, drawn by the app's own code, in each style. In every picture the big purple one is you, the green one is a guest behind you, and the small yellow one is the size of the DJ at the booth.

What each one is

  • Stick is what's live today.
  • Hands + feet is the same figure with mitten hands and shoe feet. A hand points where the fingers are; a foot runs from heel to toe.
  • Curved is a filled silhouette: a thick torso, arms that bend in a curve and taper to the wrist, tapered legs with a straight thigh and shin, small feet, and a head floating clear of the shoulders.

Try them on yourself

On branch figure-styles, the dance screen has a fourth button at the bottom, Figure, that steps through the three. It applies to you and to everyone in your space, and is remembered on the phone.

The dancer on this page is a made-up puppet, so its hands and feet are always exactly where they should be. On a real camera the finger and toe points are the least steady ones, so the phone test is what counts for hands and feet.

The drawing code on this page is the app's (src/avatar/stickFigure.js), bundled in. Rebuild with doc/figure-styles.build.sh.
